# Vali Health

> The AI workforce for home care agencies — with built-in human oversight.

Vali Health (https://vali.health) is the AI workforce for home care agencies. When a shift opens or a caregiver calls out, Vali's AI agents run caregiver outreach by text and voice, fill open shifts, update WellSky, CareSmartz360, or AxisCare, and escalate exceptions to humans — 24/7, including after-hours coverage. Founded by Serena Dang (CEO) and Jason Wu (CTO); $6M seed announced June 2026 (Bonfire Ventures, Supernode, Comma Capital; angel Jacquelyn Kung, ClearCare co-founder). Serving ~100 locations across ~30 states. Agencies report 80% fewer open shifts within two weeks, about 20 hours per week saved, and 98% of scheduling tasks handled autonomously under human oversight. HIPAA-compliant; Vali signs BAAs. Live integrations: WellSky, CareSmartz360, and AxisCare (no rip-and-replace).
